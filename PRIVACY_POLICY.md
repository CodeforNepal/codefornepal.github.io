Code for Nepal respects your privacy. This page explains what information is
collected when you visit codefornepal.org and how it is used. We do not sell
your information, and we do not ask you to create an account to read this site.

## Analytics

This website uses **Google Analytics** to help us understand how visitors use
the site, such as which pages are popular and how people find us.

Google Analytics uses **cookies**, which are small text files placed on your
device, to collect standard internet log information and visitor behavior
information in an anonymous form. This information is used only to produce
aggregate reports that help us improve the site. It is not used to identify you
personally.

You can opt out of Google Analytics by blocking cookies in your browser or by
installing the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## Information you choose to share

Some features of the site are provided by third-party services. When you use
them, the information you enter is handled by that service under its own
privacy policy:

* **Contact form**: messages sent through our contact form are delivered by
  [Formspree](https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy). We use your name,
  email address, and message only to reply to you.
* **Fellowship and program applications**: applications are collected with
  [Google Forms](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We use the information you
  submit only to review your application and to contact you about the program.
* **Blog comments**: comments on blog posts are powered by
  [Disqus](https://help.disqus.com/en/articles/1717103-disqus-privacy-policy).
* **Donations**: donations are processed by
  [PayPal](https://www.paypal.com/webapps/mpp/ua/privacy-full). We do not receive
  or store your payment details.

## Fonts and embedded content

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and may embed content such as maps,
videos, or social media posts. These providers may receive your IP address
when your browser loads that content.

## Your choices

You can block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings. Most of
this site works without cookies.

If you would like us to update or delete information you have sent us, or if
you have any questions about this policy, email us at
[contact@codefornepal.org](mailto:contact@codefornepal.org).

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on
this page.
